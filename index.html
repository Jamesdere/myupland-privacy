My Upland — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

My Upland is a hunting logbook. This explains how your data is handled.

If anything here is unclear, say so and we will fix it.

The short version

  • Sensitive data is encrypted on your phone, with a key only you hold.
  • Backing up your logbook is your choice — iCloud, My Upland, or neither.
  • Your photographs never leave your library.
  • You can delete your account and your data from inside the app, at any time.

Your account

Your account is created with your email address and a password.

We send you a message to confirm the address. It keeps the community verified and the app more secure.

Your settings

Your species picker, your colours, your season start and the rest of your preferences are stored by My Upland. Set up a new phone or restore an account and everything is as you left it.

Your logbook

Hunts, scouting trips, places, dogs, and the people you hunt with.

What is encrypted

Encrypted on your phone, with a key only you hold. Nobody else can read it:

  • Where you hunted — the exact position of a hunt, a flush or a place.
  • Everything you wrote — your notes on the day, the dogs and your gear.
  • Your counts — birds up, birds down, and what each person shot.
  • The names of the people you hunt with. They are somebody else's name, and they never agreed to be in this app.

What is not, and why

Some of it is left readable, because the app reads it to build your summaries, your insights and the predictions you take on future hunts:

  • An approximate area, about half a mile across — no more than any weather app uses to fetch your forecast. The conditions it brings back are what every insight here is built on.
  • The name you gave a place, as you typed it.
  • Dates and times, the species, and the cover.

Backing up your logbook

Three choices, and the app does not make them for you:

  • iCloud. If your phone backs up to iCloud, your logbook goes with it, like everything else on the phone.
  • My Upland. Profile → Backup → Back up my logbook. Your records are then stored by My Upland, and a new phone can restore them.
  • Neither. The logbook works exactly as it does now. It simply lives on that phone alone.

Whatever you choose, the encryption above is unchanged.

Your photographs

The app never uploads a photograph. It keeps a reference to a picture in your own photo library, and nothing else. Delete the photo from your library and it is gone — the app only ever held a pointer to it.

The weather

To work out the conditions on a hunt, the app looks up the weather for an approximate area, about half a mile across, rather than the spot you were standing on.

This is an ordinary weather request, the same one you would make looking up the forecast yourself, and it carries nothing about you: no account, no name, no identifier. It goes to Open-Meteo.

Your location

The app asks for your location to record where a hunt happened and to look up the weather there. It is read only while you are using the app — never in the background, never when it is closed.

You can refuse. The app still works, and records are logged without a position.

Deleting your data

Profile → Delete Account. It removes your account, your settings, and every record of yours stored by My Upland. It tells you what it is about to delete before it does it.

This cannot be undone, which is the point of it.

Your encryption key

The key is made on your phone. A copy is stored by My Upland, locked by your password, so that signing in on a new phone gives it back to you.

A recovery key is offered when you create an account. Forget your password with no recovery key, and the encrypted parts of your logbook cannot be recovered — not by you, not by us, not by anyone. That is what it means for the key to be yours.

Children

My Upland is not intended for children, and does not knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
